# Registered behaviour questions, Bloor St W

Written 2026-09-08, before any of the views below were looked at for "interesting" results. The point of registering them is that
the project asks the same six questions of every stretch, every month, whether or not the answer flatters anyone.
Each question names its data, its unit, and what it cannot show. New questions are appended with a date; none are removed.

| # | Question | Data | What it cannot show |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | How do people on bicycles respond to cold and rain on this stretch? | Permanent counters (daily totals, weekdays) joined to Environment Canada daily weather, 2022 onward | Why. It shows the size of the response, not whether infrastructure caused it. Counters exist on 4 of 17 stretches. |
| 2 | When do people move through here, and in which direction? | 15-minute counter bins by direction, weekdays, last 12 months; dry versus rainy weekdays by hour | Trip purpose. A morning eastbound surge is consistent with commuting, not proof of it. |
| 3 | Is the rush hour starting earlier or lasting longer at this station? | City turning-movement counts since 1984: peak-hour start times and peak-hour vehicle volumes | Congestion itself. Peak spreading is consistent with a full intersection, but count methods changed over four decades. |
| 4 | What is this stretch used for? | Latest City count at each station (vehicles × 1.25, bicycles, pedestrians) plus TTC typical-weekday station usage | Growth or decline. Counts are one day each, years apart, and subway riders are annual figures. |
| 5 | Where does the design get hit, and how? | Killed-or-seriously-injured records within 80 m of each station, grouped by impact type, road user and light; TTC delay causes by station since 2025 | Rates. Without exposure these are patterns, not risk. |
| 6 | Does traffic move to the side streets? | City counts on named parallel streets within 150 to 900 m of Bloor, grouped by stretch | Anything yet. This is a watch list: it becomes an answer only when a count after a change on Bloor can be compared with one before. |

## Rules
- Whole-street and per-stretch views use the same computation; a stretch is never given a different method to make its answer sharper.
- Sample sizes are shown on every chart. A bin with fewer than 3 days is left empty.
- Nothing here is weather- or season-normalized beyond what the question itself does. Question 1 is the normalization.
